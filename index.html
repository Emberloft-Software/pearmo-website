Why Pearmo exists

Dating apps have a trust problem.

We surveyed 93 people before writing a line of code. Women told us the same two things, over and over: they don't trust that other users are real, and they don't want their photos out there. Every app felt built around hookups. So we built the opposite.

How it works

Slow by design.

Real screens from the Pearmo app. No mockups needed — the MVP already works.

STEP 01

Answer honestly. It can't be gamed.

A psychology-based questionnaire built on the Big Five — reverse-scored, so playing it cool doesn't work. Your trait scores stay private, always.

STEP 02

Get a few curated matches a day.

No swiping, no endless grid. Compatibility does the heavy lifting and brings you a handful of people actually worth your time.

STEP 03

Break the ice before the chat.

Chat doesn't just open. Both people consent, then you unlock it by playing icebreaker games together — shared music taste included.

STEP 04

Say what you're actually here for.

Something serious? Say it up front. Pearmo profiles lead with intent and values — ambition, emotional depth — not gym selfies.

Games first. Chat second.

Icebreakers turn “hey” into an actual conversation — you already know you click before the first message.

Safety, not vibes

Real people. Really verified.

Anonymity for you doesn't mean anonymity for bad actors. Every layer of Pearmo assumes trust has to be earned by the platform first.

Liveness + ID verification

A selfie liveness check and national ID verification, so the person and the age are both real. Verified badges you can actually believe.

Your photos stay private

No public photo grid. You show up as your avatar, and your real photos are shared only when you choose, with who you choose.

Consent gates everything

Chat, photo reveals, contact details — nothing moves forward unless both people opt in. Slowing down is the feature.
